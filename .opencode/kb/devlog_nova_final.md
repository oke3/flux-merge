## The Void was winning.

Imagine your board is a graveyard of high-level nodes, but the center is a hungry, expanding abyss. The Void doesn't just consume; it stagnates. When the consumption rate outpaces the merge rate, the game stops being a puzzle and starts being a countdown to inevitable erasure.

---

### The Entropy Trap

You've felt it. That moment where a single Black Hole anchors the center of the map, and every move you make only feeds the machine. The traditional "get bigger" strategy fails when the map itself is disappearing. We had a system of destruction, but we lacked a system of resistance.

### The Pivot: Refraction over Resistance

Most developers would just nerf the Void. Lower the radius. Slow the speed. But that removes the threat. To keep the tension, we needed a counter-force that didn't just "stop" the Void, but leveraged its hunger.

Enter the **Luminous Nova**.

### The Nova Mechanic: Swell and Snap

The Nova isn't a static wall. It's a living, breathing pulsar. It operates on a rhythmic "Swell-Snap" cycle:

*   **The Swell (1.5s):** A gradual, sinusoidal expansion of golden light. It warns the board that a pulse is coming.
*   **The Snap (0.2s):** An exponential burst of energy that hits the maximum repulsion radius.
*   **The Repulsion:** Any node caught in the snap is violently pushed away from the Nova's center, disrupting Void clusters and creating breathing room for merges.

> **Technical Insight: The Refraction Effect**
> The Nova's true power is Refraction. When a Void node attempts to consume a target already within the Nova's protective aura, the consumption is intercepted. Instead of being erased, the target node "shatters" into several smaller, lower-level shards. We've effectively turned a death sentence into a redistribution of resources.

### From Erasure to Expansion

In our latest adversarial tests, the Nova changed the game state. A board that was 70% Void-consumed was reclaimed. By timing the "Snap" to hit a Void-cluster, players can now shatter the "un-mergeable" high-level nodes and reset the local entropy.

The result? The game doesn't just last longer; it becomes more volatile.
