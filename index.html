Cosmic Guide

Basics of Flux Merge

The goal of the game is to merge nodes of the same level to create higher-level nodes. To merge, simply drag one node on top of another of the same color.

The ultimate objective is to reach the Singularity, which is a Level 5 node. Achieving this wins the game.

Understanding Nodes

Standard Nodes: The core elements of the game. Merge two Level 1s to get a Level 2, and so on.

Pulsars (Cyan): These nodes emit a pulse that pushes all other nearby nodes away, making it harder to merge them.

Prisms (Magenta): Unstable elements. Instead of merging normally, they split into two lower-level nodes upon collision.

Voids (Black): Dangerous singularities that consume any standard node that gets too close, removing it from the board.

Progression and Strategy

Every successful merge grants you Experience Points (XP). You can use this XP in your Cosmic Profile to upgrade abilities like Magnetic Pull, which helps attract nodes together.

Strategic Tip: Manage your space carefully. Since new nodes spawn over time, keeping your board clear of clusters is key to avoiding a Game Over.
